Swallows

Soft doughs you pinch, dip in soup and swallow. Each comes with a soup or stew.

  • Fufu Egusi

    Fufu with egusi stew made from ground melon seeds.

    • Fufu With Goat Egusi Stew

  • Banku

    Fermented corn and cassava dough, stirred smooth. Classic with okro soup or fish.

    • Banku With Okro Soup Goat Meat

  • Kenkey

    Fermented corn dough, wrapped and steamed. Firm and pleasantly sour, often eaten with fried fish and pepper.

  • Eba

    Made from gari (toasted cassava granules) stirred with hot water into a soft dough.

  • Pounded Yam

    Boiled yam pounded until smooth and elastic. Mild, and great with egusi.

Rice & beans

Fork-friendly plates and an easy first order.

  • Jollof

    Rice cooked in a spiced tomato and pepper base.

    • Jollof Rice w/ Chicken, Fish, Egg, Plantain
    • Jollof rice with beef and vegetables

  • Waakye

    Rice and beans cooked together until deep reddish-brown, with a spread of sides.

    • Waakye W/ Fish, Chicken, Egg, Plantains
    • Waakye W(2) Goat Meat, Egg, Sweet Plantains

  • Rice

    Rice plates paired with a stew of your choice.

    • Rice With Cassava Leaf Stew

  • Beans

    Bean dishes, a West African staple, often paired with plantain.

Yam, plantain & attiéké

Starchy, satisfying bases that are not rice.

  • Yam

    Boiled yam, sliced thick, with a pepper stew.

    • Boiled yam with fish in pepper stew, avocado and boiled eggs

  • Green Plantain

    Unripe plantain, boiled or cooked savory rather than sweet.

  • Attiéké (Atteke)

    Attiéké: steamed, fermented cassava grains, light and fluffy like couscous.

    • Atieke with fried tilapia, onions and tomatoes
