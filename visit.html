FAQ

Visiting questions

Is Lizzy's open on Mondays?

No. Lizzy's is closed on Mondays and open Tuesday through Sunday.

What time does Lizzy's open on Sunday?

Sunday hours are 1 PM to 8 PM.

Can I order ahead for pickup?

Yes. Call (804) 716-4259 to order ahead, then pick up at 5310 Chamberlayne Rd.

Does Lizzy's deliver?

Yes, through DoorDash and Grubhub. Delivery-app menus and prices can differ from in-store.

Where do I park?
